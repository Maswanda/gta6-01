HEATWAVE — an open-world crime story

Jax Marlowe hung up the getaway wheel years ago. Then Mara Voss called from the pier, and Solmar Bay pulled him back in. Twelve jobs stand between him and the Last Run — steal cars, outrun the PD, run the jobs, race the beachfront and find the city's hidden coins along the way. At four stars the PD sends up a helicopter, and only the safehouse or a good shot brings it down. Between jobs the city keeps moving: carjackings, police pursuits and ATM robberies break out on their own, Rico sells cars at the garage, the radio plays three stations, and the sun sets over the Marina while you work.
